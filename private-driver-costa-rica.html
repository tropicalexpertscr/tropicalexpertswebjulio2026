FAQ

Private driver in Costa Rica: frequent questions

What is the minimum number of days?

The private driver service is booked for at least 3 consecutive days. For a single trip from one place to another, book a private transfer instead.

How much does a private driver cost in Costa Rica?

One flat rate per vehicle per day, taxes included: $365 for the Hyundai Staria (1–5 passengers), $450 for the Toyota Hiace (6–9) and $545 for the Maxus V90 (10–12). For example, 3 days in the Staria cost $1,095.

How many hours a day does the driver work for us?

The daily rate includes 12 hours of service in a row, counted from your daily start time (for example, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.). You choose a start time between 3:00 and 10:00 a.m. Service always ends by 10:00 p.m., because that is the driver's rest time, so extra hours are only possible until then: with a 6:00 a.m. start you can add up to 4 extra hours a day, with 7:00 a.m. up to 3, and with a 10:00 a.m. start the 12 hours already end at 10:00 p.m. and none can be added. Extra hours cost $35 each; you can add them when you book or, if your plan needs more during the trip, we send you a payment link (always within the 10:00 p.m. limit).

Is there a daily mileage limit?

Yes. The rate includes 105 miles (169 km) per day, which is about the distance from San José airport to Limón or to Manuel Antonio. Distance beyond that is charged at $1 per mile (about $0.62 per km), measured on the vehicle's odometer and paid by a payment link we send you. The driver never collects any payment. The service is meant for touring routes, not for very long trips such as driving to the Panama border from San José airport.

Is it the same driver for the whole trip?

Yes. The same driver and the same vehicle stay with you from the first pick-up to the last drop-off.

Are fuel, tolls and the driver's expenses included?

Yes. Fuel, tolls and taxes are included, and we cover the driver's lodging and meals. You never pay separately for fuel, tolls or the driver's lodging and meals.

Can we go somewhere different each day?

Yes. It is a flexible service, not a fixed tour: each day can follow a different route, within a reasonable daily itinerary. Send us your plan day by day when you book; if something changes during the trip, message us on WhatsApp.

Can we add the VIP upgrade?

Yes, and you choose the days. Tropical Experts VIP is +$80 for each VIP day. Each VIP day includes 2 extra hours of service (worth $70), a guaranteed bilingual driver who is also your guide, sightseeing stops and a welcome kit. You can add VIP to all your days or only to some of them. Because the 2 extra hours must also end by 10:00 p.m., VIP days need a start time of 8:00 a.m. or earlier. The Standard service does not guarantee a bilingual driver.

How do I pay?

In full when you book, by card (Visa, Mastercard or Amex) on Tilopay's secure payment page. Your card details never pass through our site. You receive your order number and a PDF voucher by email.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the first pick-up.

Only need one transfer?

For a single trip, from the airport to your hotel or between two destinations, see our private transfer prices.
