        Our story

        Meet the local experts, a 100% Costa Rican family

        My name is Johnson Paniagua Berrocal, and Tropical Experts Costa Rica is the family company I founded in 2023 together with my wife and co-founder, Andrea Segura. We live in La Fortuna de San Carlos with our two daughters, at the foot of Arenal volcano, and from here we lead a team of professional drivers covering the whole country.

        I have spent more than 21 years in tourism, working in all kinds of places: canopy guide at Sky Trek, rafting guide and naturalist guide. I then moved into five-star hotels such as Tabacón and Arenal Kioro, in guest reception, concierge and customer service, where I also trained new staff. I know what a traveller needs because I have worked in every link of their trip.

        And there is one chapter of my life that defines how we work: I was a detective with the Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ) for 8 years. That is where the values this company does not negotiate come from — safety, discipline, attention to detail and punctuality. For us, transporting someone is not moving them from one point to another: it is taking responsibility for our clients arriving safe, comfortable and on time.

        Today we have more than 1,200 transfers completed, with modern vehicles with reclining seats, our tourist transport permits in force, travel insurance and I.C.T. licence #3946-2025 (Instituto Costarricense de Turismo, the country's tourism regulator). Our mission is simple: to make your holiday the best it can be, and for you not merely to reach your destination, but to enjoy the road there.
