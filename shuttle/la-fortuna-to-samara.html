The service

Your private transfer from La Fortuna to Sámara

Shared shuttles run to a timetable and stop at several hotels. With us the van is booked for your party only: you set the departure time from La Fortuna, the driver waits while you check out, and the drive to Sámara goes door to door.

What you see is what you pay, per vehicle: fuel, tolls, taxes, insurance, water, WiFi and child seats are all in the price. Standard is the direct trip; VIP adds $80 per vehicle and turns the drive into a day with stops.

Your destination

Arriving in Sámara

Sámara is a relaxed beach village on the Pacific side of the Nicoya Peninsula, set on a wide bay protected by an offshore reef. The reef keeps the water calm for much of the year, which makes Sámara one of the best beaches in Guanacaste for families, swimmers and beginner surfers, with Isla Chora sitting in the middle of the bay.

The village has a lively little centre with restaurants, surf schools and shops right by the beach, and quieter areas to the south towards Playa Carrillo. Among the places we drive to are Hotel Villas Playa Sámara and Samara Treehouse Inn.

We confirm the pickup time with you when you book. With approximately 4 h 45 min of driving ahead, most travellers leave La Fortuna in the morning so they arrive in daylight.

The route

What to expect on the drive

Main roads

Last stretch
From Nicoya, the paved road over the hills down to Sámara

Beyond the bridge the road runs through the town of Nicoya and then out towards the Pacific on smaller roads, through hills of dry forest and cattle farms. This is the part of the trip where the time goes, because the last stretch is slower than the distance suggests.

From the town of Nicoya a paved road crosses the hills of the peninsula and drops down to the coast at Sámara, with the bay appearing below in the final kilometres.

Before you travel

Road conditions and 2026 roadworks

On the peninsula the main roads are paved, but some of the access roads to the beaches are gravel, dusty in the dry season and muddy in the wet one. All our vehicles drive gravel roads; in the rainy season, river crossings and potholes can slow the last stretch.

The road from Nicoya to Sámara is paved and in reasonable condition, which makes Sámara one of the easier beach destinations on the peninsula. Some streets and lanes in and around the village are gravel.

And if a road closure or extreme weather makes the transfer impossible, our terms let you choose: a new date or your money back in full.

From the road

Driver's tips for this route

  1. Bring cash in colones for small purchases on arrival; beach towns on the peninsula have fewer ATMs.
  2. If you have small children, Sámara's calm water is ideal; ask locally about the tides before swimming far out.
  3. Kayaking to Isla Chora is a classic first-day plan on a calm morning.
  4. Add your WhatsApp number when you book; it is the quickest way for the driver to reach you on the day.

Questions

Frequently asked questions: La Fortuna → Sámara

How long is the drive from La Fortuna to Sámara?

Approximately 4 h 45 min of driving according to our fare table, for about 210 km. Stops, rain and traffic come on top of that; with VIP, add the one to two hours of sightseeing stops.

How much is a private shuttle from La Fortuna to Sámara?

$370 in a Hyundai Staria (1–5 passengers), $415 in a Toyota Hiace (6–9) and $500 in a Maxus V90 (10–12). Prices are per vehicle, in US dollars, with taxes included. VIP adds $80 per vehicle.

Do you take a ferry to the Nicoya Peninsula?

Not on this route: from Arenal we cross to the peninsula by road, over the Tempisque bridge on Route 18. It is the most direct way from the north.

Is the road to Sámara paved?

Yes, the main road from Nicoya to Sámara is paved. Some local streets are gravel, and all our vehicles drive them.

Is Sámara good for families?

It is one of the calmest bays in Guanacaste thanks to the offshore reef, so it is popular with families. Conditions change with tides and seasons; check locally before swimming.

Can you drop us at Playa Carrillo instead of Sámara?

Yes. Carrillo is a few minutes south of Sámara along the coast road. Give us the address when you book and we confirm the fare and the time.
