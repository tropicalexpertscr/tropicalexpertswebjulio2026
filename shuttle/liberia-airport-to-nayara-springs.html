After immigration at Daniel Oduber airport, walk outside and look for your name on our driver's sign. The route goes south on the Inter-American Highway to Cañas, inland to Tilarán, and then along the north shore of the lake, the slowest and most scenic part of the trip.

Past the dam, Route 142 runs down the volcano's flank toward La Fortuna. The Nayara entrance comes up on this stretch, about 9 km before the town center, so you never drive through La Fortuna itself. Forbes Travel Guide lists Liberia at about 2 h 40 min from the hotel; our own schedule uses 2 h 45 min.

A practical point for honeymooners: Liberia flights often land around midday, so the lake section is usually driven in good afternoon light. Ask your driver to point out the dam and the wind turbines above Tilarán; you will be on the villa terrace before dark on most days of the year.

The hotel

Arriving at an adults-only villa

Every villa at Nayara Springs has its own plunge pool filled with mineral spring water, and the hotel describes the villas as independent, single-storey units in the rainforest. Guests must be 18 or older, which makes it a frequent choice for honeymoons and anniversaries.

Dinner is close: Mis Amores and Amor Loco are among the four restaurants and bars Forbes counts on site, and a footbridge leads to more options at Nayara Gardens. The Michelin Guide gives Nayara Springs three Keys, its highest distinction for hotels.

FAQ

Liberia ↔ Nayara Springs: questions

How much is the Liberia airport to Nayara Springs shuttle?

One price for the vehicle, not per person: $225 for up to five. Add the return for $225 and you pay $450 for both legs.

How long is the drive from LIR to Nayara Springs?

About 2 h 45 min via Cañas, Tilarán and the north shore of Lake Arenal. The Nayara complex is on Route 142 before La Fortuna, so there is no town traffic at the end.

Do you drive to Nayara Springs or to the Nayara complex entrance?

To Nayara Springs. The complex has three hotels; tell us which one is on your reservation and we drop you at the right reception.

Can we stop at the lake on the way?

VIP adds $80 per vehicle and one to two hours of stops by the lake.

Is it worth flying into Liberia for Nayara Springs?

If your flights to Liberia are better, yes: the transfer is approx. 2 h 45 min and costs $225, close to the SJO fare. Liberia also makes sense if you continue to the Guanacaste beaches.
