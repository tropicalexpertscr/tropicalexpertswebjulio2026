From SJO there is no way to Tabacón that avoids the mountains. The driver chooses between Route 702 through San Ramón and La Tigra and Route 141 through Zarcero and Ciudad Quesada, based on traffic and weather that day; both end in La Fortuna after roughly three hours.

Then the volcano takes over the windshield. Route 142 leaves town westward, passes the hotel strip and climbs gently along the base of Arenal. Tabacón is on this road, between 11 and 13 km out depending on where you measure from, with its sign half hidden in tropical plants. Allow a little extra time after La Fortuna for that last run.

Tabacón is also part of our story: our founder has more than 21 years in tourism, including guest reception and concierge at five-star hotels such as Tabacón and Arenal Kioro, so the resort's arrival routine is familiar territory for our team.

The resort

The thermal river after a travel day

Tabacón's centerpiece is a natural thermal river that flows from the base of the volcano and forms waterfalls and pools between 72 and 100°F (22 to 38°C), according to the resort. Hotel guests have unlimited access to it, and Shangri-La Gardens is an adults-only area reserved for them.

There is a swim-up bar in the springs, Agua Ardiente, and restaurants that range from Ave for a relaxed meal to Tucanes for tasting menus in the evening. The resort is a member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World and is selected in the Michelin Guide. Arrive by late afternoon and the river at dusk is the first thing on the agenda.

FAQ

SJO ↔ Tabacón Thermal Resort: questions

How much does a private transfer from SJO to Tabacón cost?

$220 per vehicle for one to five passengers, taxes and tolls included. Larger groups: $275 in the Hiace or $360 in the Maxus. Going home costs $220 for up to five.

Is Tabacón far from La Fortuna?

Between 11 and 13 km west, on Route 142 toward the lake. The ICT tourism portal counts 11 km from the La Fortuna church; Michelin and Travel Weekly say 13.

How long is the drive from SJO to Tabacón?

About 3 h to La Fortuna plus the short run west to the resort. Rain or fog in the mountains, or traffic on Route 1, can stretch it.

Can we stop at La Paz Waterfall Gardens on the way?

Yes. VIP costs $80 per vehicle and the stops are listed above.

What time should we leave Tabacón for an afternoon flight?

For a 3 p.m. flight from SJO the table suggests about 8:00 a.m. We confirm the exact time when you book, based on your flight number.
