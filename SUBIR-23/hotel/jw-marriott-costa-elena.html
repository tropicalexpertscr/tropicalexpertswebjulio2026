Marriott opened this resort on 30 September 2026 as the first all-inclusive in the JW Marriott brand, so it is a new hotel. Several sources note that it occupies the site of the former Dreams Las Mareas. Reports describe 415 rooms and suites, 17 pools, 15 dining and bar venues, a wellness area of about 16,000 square feet and a Family by JW Club. The Griffin Club is a premium tier within the resort, with its own lounge, pool and beach area. Marriott lists the property as family-friendly, with a minimum check-in age of 18.

The location is the main thing to know before booking a transfer. El Jobo is a small community on a peninsula between Bahía Salinas and the Gulf of Santa Elena, and the nearest town is La Cruz. Marriott states that the hotel has no shuttle service, and one travel guide to the resort warns that Arenal, Monteverde and La Fortuna are multi-hour drives from here, with no dining or nightlife outside the resort. Our fare zone, La Cruz · Costa Elena, is priced separately from Papagayo for that reason.

Do not confuse it with the other JW Marriott in Guanacaste. A separate JW Marriott Guanacaste Resort & Spa stands near Tamarindo, at Hacienda Pinilla, and it is a different hotel on the Pacific side. When you book with us, give the full name, JW Marriott Costa Elena, so the voucher and driver point to El Jobo.

From the airports

Getting to JW Marriott Costa Elena from SJO and Liberia

From SJO airport (San José)

From San José your driver waits outside arrivals with a sign bearing your name, and we follow your flight. Plan on approx. 5 h of driving, the longest transfer in this part of the country and a full day after an international flight. The route runs west on Route 1, the Inter-American Highway, through Cañas and Liberia, and then continues north to La Cruz before the final stretch to the bay. If your itinerary allows it, a Liberia arrival saves hours. If you stay with San José, ask for stops; a VIP transfer adds one to two hours of sightseeing, and the Standard goes direct with a restroom break if you need one.

From Liberia airport (LIR)

Liberia is the airport for this resort. Marriott lists the hotel about 90 miles away, a figure that does not match other sources, which give around 54 to 56 miles (86 to 90 km). Plan on approx. 1 h of driving, while a travel agency and a hotel review each speak of about an hour and a half, so plan on the longer time. The route follows Route 1 north from Liberia to La Cruz, turns off through the town and descends toward Bahía Salinas, then passes Playa Papaturro and Playa Copal before reaching El Jobo. The last stretch is a winding descent. The fare is $160 for up to five passengers, $215 for up to nine and $300 for up to twelve.

Before you go

Practical notes for JW Marriott Costa Elena

  1. The resort opened in late September 2026. Confirm with the hotel the services and hours you care about, such as restaurant reservations and the Griffin Club, before you travel, because a new property is still settling in.
  2. Check-in at JW Marriott Costa Elena starts at 3:00 pm and check-out is at noon, per Marriott. Since the trip from Liberia is long, an early arrival may mean waiting for the room; ask the hotel how it handles that.
  3. Add the margin. The drive from the airport is closer to 1.5 hours than 1, and the descent to El Jobo is winding. Daylight makes the last stretch easier, so a flight that lands before late afternoon is the comfortable choice.
  4. Moving on after your stay? We collect you at the hotel at zone fares: $240 to Papagayo, $240 to Tamarindo, $290 to Monteverde or $330 to La Fortuna for up to five passengers.

Questions

Questions about transfers to JW Marriott Costa Elena

How much is a private shuttle from SJO or Liberia airport to JW Marriott Costa Elena?

From SJO: $340 (Staria, 1–5 passengers), $385 (Hiace, 6–9) or $470 (Maxus, 10–12). From Liberia: $160, $215 or $300. Per vehicle, taxes included; the return costs the same.

Is JW Marriott Costa Elena on the Papagayo peninsula?

No. It is on Playa El Jobo in the La Cruz district, in the far north of Guanacaste. Papagayo is a separate area and a separate fare zone. A transfer from the hotel to Papagayo costs $240 for up to five passengers.

How long is the drive from Liberia airport to JW Marriott Costa Elena?

Plan on approx. 1 h of driving. A Costa Rica travel agency and a hotel review each describe about an hour and a half, and The Points Guy gives one to two hours, so allow the longer time. The route runs north on Route 1 to La Cruz and then down toward Bahía Salinas and El Jobo.

Is this the same hotel as the JW Marriott Guanacaste near Tamarindo?

No. The JW Marriott Guanacaste Resort & Spa is at Hacienda Pinilla, near Tamarindo. JW Marriott Costa Elena is a newer, separate hotel at El Jobo, near La Cruz, opened in September 2026. Please give us the full name when you book.

Is it worth flying into San José for this hotel?

Plan on approx. 5 h from SJO, against approx. 1 h from Liberia, and the fare is $340 against $160 for up to five passengers. Liberia is the practical airport. If your flights go through San José, we will drive you; we recommend a rest stop on the way.

Will the driver take us to the hotel entrance? There is no hotel shuttle.

Yes. Marriott states the hotel does not run a shuttle, so a private transfer is how most guests arrive. The driver takes you to the resort itself, not to the road. Give us your flight and the name on your reservation and we meet you at arrivals.

Can we make stops on the way, or visit another destination later?

With Tropical Experts VIP (+$80 per vehicle) you get one to two hours of stops along the route. Extra hours are $35 each, up to 4. For onward travel, the zone fare is $240 to Tamarindo or $290 to Monteverde for up to five passengers.

How do I book a transfer to JW Marriott Costa Elena?

To book online, type “JW Marriott Costa Elena” in the calculator (it appears under La Cruz · Costa Elena), then choose your date and vehicle. You pay by card through Tilopay, and you can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before pickup.
