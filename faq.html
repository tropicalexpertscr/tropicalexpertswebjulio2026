FAQ

Prices and booking

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. Whether two or five people travel in the Hyundai Staria, the price is identical. So the more of you travel, the less each person pays.

Does the price I see already include tax?

Yes. Every fare on this site is a final price with all taxes already included, as applies to tourism services registered with the ICT. Nothing is added at checkout: what you are quoted is what you pay. Your electronic invoice shows the VAT broken out, as Costa Rican tax law requires.

Are tolls included?

Yes. Tolls and taxes are always included in the price per vehicle; there is nothing to pay the driver.

How do I book my transfer?

Search your route in the calculator, choose the vehicle and fill in your trip details: route, date, time, flight and hotel. Your TECR order number is created instantly, you pay securely by card and you receive your PDF voucher by email.

How and when do I pay?

When you book, the site takes you to Tilopay's secure platform to pay the full amount by card (Visa, Mastercard or Amex) in US dollars; once the payment is approved, your booking is confirmed and you receive your PDF voucher by email. The price on the site is exactly what you pay: no surcharges and nothing to pay the driver. Your card details are never entered on our site: they are processed and protected by Tilopay, a PCI DSS-certified gateway with 3-D Secure verification. If you prefer SINPE Móvil or a bank transfer, message us on WhatsApp and we send you the instructions.

What is the cancellation window?

Up to 48 hours before pick-up, with a full refund. Within 48 hours there is no refund, except for a documented flight cancellation.

How far ahead should I book, especially in high season?

Ideally a week; from December to April and in July, when demand rises, two or three weeks is better. The site takes bookings with as little as 12 hours' notice. If your trip leaves in under 12 hours, before charging you we offer to confirm on WhatsApp that a vehicle is available: we reply within minutes, and we can often make it work. Trips from December 15 to 31 are booked on WhatsApp only because of high-season demand: the site writes the message with all your trip details and we confirm availability.

What is the difference between Standard and VIP?

The standard transfer goes door to door by the fastest route. Tropical Experts VIP (+$80 per vehicle) adds one to two hours of sightseeing stops, a guaranteed bilingual driver and a welcome kit on board; each extra hour is $35, up to 4.

FAQ

Airports and flights

Do you pick up at both SJO and Liberia (LIR) airports?

Yes. SJO (Juan Santamaría, San José) and LIR (Daniel Oduber, Liberia). For example, SJO to La Fortuna is $220 and Liberia to La Fortuna is $225 for up to 5 passengers.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight number you give us. If you land late, the driver adjusts with no additional charge. All we ask is that you enter the flight number when booking.

How will I recognize my driver at the airport?

Your driver waits for you at arrivals, holding a sign with your name. The day before your trip we send you the driver's name, the plate number and a 24/7 contact.

Do you drive at night?

Yes. We pick up 24 hours a day at no extra charge, including late-night arrivals.

FAQ

On the road

Is the service private or shared?

Private. The vehicle is only for your group: you choose the pick-up time and we take you straight to your door.

Can I stop along the way?

The standard transfer is direct: door to door by the fastest route, with a restroom stop if you need one. To get out and explore — lookouts, waterfalls, a coffee farm — there is Tropical Experts VIP: one to two hours of sightseeing stops with time to enjoy them, the driver who knows the area and recommends the best stops, and a welcome kit on board. If you want to stretch the outing further, each extra hour is 35 dollars and is added from the calculator.

Are child seats included?

Yes, at no cost. Tell us the ages and weights in the booking notes and we bring the correct seat already fitted before we pick you up.

How much luggage fits?

The Staria takes 5 large bags, the Hiace 9 and the Maxus 12, plus carry-on bags. If you travel with surfboards, strollers or diving gear, tell us and we assign the right unit.

Do your drivers speak English?

Our team speaks Spanish and English. If you want a bilingual driver guaranteed for your transfer, choose Tropical Experts VIP.

Is it safe to ride with you?

We are an authorized tourist transport operator with I.C.T. license #3946-2025 (also regulated by the CTP under the same number), liability insurance and professional drivers. Our founder spent 8 years as a detective with Costa Rica's Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ): safety and punctuality are how we work.

Still have a question?

Write to us on WhatsApp: a person from our team answers, any hour of the day.
