Travel Weekly lists Los Lagos 6 km from the village of La Fortuna and places it in its First Class category; a Costa Rica travel guide that reviewed it describes it as budget-friendly and located just off the main road to Lake Arenal. In our calculator it is listed simply as “Hotel Los Lagos”.

At 6 km, Los Lagos is in the first half of the Route 142 hotel strip. The last part of any transfer is therefore short, and the fare is the standard La Fortuna one. For families and groups, the Hiace and the Maxus carry up to nine and twelve passengers with their luggage.

From the airports

Getting to Hotel Los Lagos from SJO and Liberia

From SJO airport (San José)

Leaving SJO, the driver follows Route 1 west and crosses to the northern lowlands on Route 702 or Route 141, reaching La Fortuna in approx. 3 h according to our fare table. Los Lagos is about 6 km further, just off Route 142. VIP transfers take the Vara Blanca road instead, for La Paz waterfall and the Cinchona lookout.

From Liberia airport (LIR)

From Liberia the lake road enters the La Fortuna area from the west on Route 142, so Los Lagos comes before the village. It is approx. 2 h 45 min of driving in our fare table, the slowest part being the curves along the north shore of Lake Arenal.

Before you go

Practical notes for Hotel Los Lagos

  1. Travelling as a family of six to nine? The Toyota Hiace is $275 from SJO and $280 from Liberia for the whole group.
  2. Child seats are free; tell us the ages and weights when you book so the seats are fitted before pickup.
  3. Planning Monteverde after Arenal? From Los Lagos it is $255 for up to five passengers.
  4. Arriving from Liberia in the late afternoon? The lake road has almost no street lights, so the last hour may be in the dark; keep a jacket and a phone charger in your hand luggage.

Questions

Questions about transfers to Hotel Los Lagos

How much is a private shuttle from SJO or Liberia airport to Hotel Los Lagos?

From SJO: $220 (Staria, 1–5 passengers), $275 (Hiace, 6–9) or $360 (Maxus, 10–12). From Liberia: $225, $280 or $365. Per vehicle, taxes included; the return costs the same.

Where is Hotel Los Lagos?

About 6 km from the village of La Fortuna, just off Route 142, the main road to Lake Arenal.

How much is a transfer from SJO to Los Lagos for a family of six?

Six passengers travel in the Toyota Hiace, which takes up to nine people and nine large bags: $275 from SJO, per vehicle, taxes included.

Is it better to fly into SJO or Liberia for Los Lagos?

Either works. SJO is approx. 3 h and $220; Liberia is approx. 2 h 45 min and $225. From Liberia you reach Los Lagos before La Fortuna village.

Do we pay the driver anything on arrival?

No. The fare is paid in full when you book, by card through Tilopay (SINPE Móvil or bank transfer can be arranged on WhatsApp). Tolls, fuel and taxes are included.

Can you take us from Los Lagos to the Caribbean coast?

Yes. Puerto Viejo is $390 for up to five passengers; the driving time is in the table above.

What happens if the airline cancels our flight to SJO?

We move your transfer to the new date at no charge. Otherwise, cancellations within 48 hours of pickup are not refunded, except for a flight cancellation documented by the airline.

How do I book a transfer to Hotel Los Lagos?

To book online, type “Hotel Los Lagos” in the calculator (it appears under La Fortuna · Arenal), then choose your date and vehicle. You pay by card through Tilopay, and you can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before pickup.
