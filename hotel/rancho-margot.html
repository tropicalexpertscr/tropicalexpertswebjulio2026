Rancho Margot's own directions take you through El Castillo and El Fósforo, with the final 2.5 km on a gravel road. It describes itself as a sustainable eco-lodge. It is one of the farthest of the Arenal lodges we list, beyond the El Castillo hotels, which is why its fare is La Fortuna +$40 per vehicle, twice the surcharge of El Castillo.

The online calculator doesn't include that surcharge yet, so bookings are made on WhatsApp. Send us your dates, flight and number of passengers, and tell us about your luggage. All our vehicles handle the gravel access road.

From the airports

Getting to Rancho Margot from SJO and Liberia

From SJO airport (San José)

From SJO the route crosses the Central Valley on Route 1 and the mountains on Route 702 or Route 141 to the La Fortuna area, approx. 3 h of driving to La Fortuna in our fare table. From there it continues west on Route 142, turns toward El Castillo and carries on past El Fósforo to the lodge, with the gravel section at the end.

From Liberia airport (LIR)

From Liberia the lake road leads to Route 142 on the western side of the volcano, where the turn-off toward El Castillo comes before La Fortuna town. Our fare table gives approx. 2 h 45 min to La Fortuna; the trip to Rancho Margot then depends on the last stretch past El Castillo, which we plan with you when you book.

Questions

Questions about transfers to Rancho Margot

How much is a private shuttle from SJO or Liberia airport to Rancho Margot?

From SJO: $260 (Staria, 1–5 passengers), $315 (Hiace, 6–9) or $400 (Maxus, 10–12). From Liberia: $265, $320 or $405. Per vehicle, taxes included; the return costs the same. They include the +$40 that applies to Rancho Margot.

Why is Rancho Margot +$40?

It is beyond El Castillo, with the last 2.5 km on gravel. Its fare is La Fortuna +$40 per vehicle: $260 from SJO and $265 from Liberia for up to five passengers.

Can I book Rancho Margot online?

Not yet: the calculator doesn't include its surcharge. Message us on WhatsApp with your dates, flight and group size and we book it with you.

How long is the trip from SJO to Rancho Margot?

Our fare table gives approx. 3 h from SJO to La Fortuna; add the drive west to El Castillo and on past El Fósforo, including the gravel section. We give you an estimate for your date when you book.

Is the road to Rancho Margot paved?

Not all of it. According to the lodge's directions, the final 2.5 km are gravel.

Can you take us from Rancho Margot to the beach?

Yes. With the +$40 included, it is $355 to Tamarindo, $325 to the Papagayo Peninsula and $370 to Manuel Antonio for up to five passengers.

How do we pay if we book on WhatsApp?
