Planet Hollywood Costa Rica was built in 2018, according to the property listings we read, and stands on Playa Manzanillo on Bahía Culebra. The hotel's own website lists 292 suites, and Marriott files it under the Autograph Collection with an all-suites, all-inclusive format. Marriott also lists seven restaurants, among them a steakhouse, a Japanese teppanyaki and sushi venue, an Italian trattoria and a Tex-Mex cantina. The resort is aimed at families as much as couples: there is no minimum age to stay, a kids club for ages 4 to 12, and a premium STAR Class tier with its own lounge and pool areas.

That mix shapes the transfer. Parties are often larger than at a boutique hotel, with grandparents, children and a lot of luggage, so it pays to choose the right vehicle size. Our fares cover up to five passengers, six to nine, and ten to twelve, per vehicle, and child seats can be arranged on request.

Neither the all-inclusive rate nor the hotel page we read says that an airport transfer is part of it. In fact, Marriott's listing states that the hotel does not run a shuttle and points to a paid third-party service. If your package from a tour operator mentions transfers, check what it covers before you book separately.

From the airports

Getting to Planet Hollywood Costa Rica from SJO and Liberia

From SJO airport (San José)

The drive from San José follows Route 1 north through Cañas to Liberia, and from there a local road leads west to the Gulf of Papagayo. Our fare table gives approx. 4 h 15 min of driving. It is a long day for a family, so ask the driver for a restroom stop whenever the children need one; water is on board. Check-in starts at 15:00 according to Marriott and the booking listings, so plan the pickup around that.

From Liberia airport (LIR)

From Liberia airport the fare table gives approx. 30 min. Sources disagree on the drive: Marriott says the resort is 31.2 km away, the resort's own site says about 30 minutes, and other listings quote 40 or even 48 minutes, so we plan with the table figure and add margin for the last stretch of local road. The fare is $110 for up to five passengers, $165 for six to nine and $250 for ten to twelve, tolls and taxes included.

Before you go

Practical notes for Planet Hollywood Costa Rica

  1. Give us the name on the reservation and your package type. The resort appears as Planet Hollywood Costa Rica, and some listings add the words by Royalton.
  2. A listing on hotels.com says check-in runs from 15:00 until midnight and asks guests arriving after midnight to contact the property in advance. If your flight is late, send the resort your flight number before you leave home.
  3. Travelling with small children? Tell us how many seats you need and the children's ages when you book, so the right ones are in the vehicle.
  4. A family of six or more needs the nine-passenger vehicle: the fare is $165 from Liberia, for the whole vehicle.
  5. After the resort, Rincón de la Vieja is $260 for up to five passengers and Monteverde is $275, both collected at the resort.

Questions

Questions about transfers to Planet Hollywood Costa Rica

How much is a private shuttle from SJO or Liberia airport to Planet Hollywood Costa Rica?

From SJO: $340 (Staria, 1–5 passengers), $385 (Hiace, 6–9) or $470 (Maxus, 10–12). From Liberia: $110, $165 or $250. Per vehicle, taxes included; the return costs the same.

How long is the drive from Liberia airport to Planet Hollywood Costa Rica?

Our fare table gives approx. 30 min. Published figures vary between about 30 and 48 minutes, which is why we add margin. Immigration and baggage at LIR can take longer than the drive.

Does the resort include an airport transfer in the all-inclusive rate?

We found no source saying so. Marriott's listing states that the hotel does not provide shuttle service. Check your package, and if no transfer is included, we can pick you up at the airport at the fare shown on this page.

We are a family with young children. Can you provide car seats?

Yes, child seats are available on request. Tell us how many children are travelling and their ages when you book, so the right seats are ready in the vehicle.

Our flight lands after dark. Can we still check in?

We follow your flight and the driver adjusts to the real landing time. One booking site lists check-in from 15:00 until midnight and asks guests who will arrive later to contact the property first, so inform the resort of your flight number.

We are travelling with golf bags and several large suitcases. Is that a problem?

It is a question of vehicle size. Tell us the number of passengers and bags when you book, and we will recommend the five-, nine- or twelve-passenger option. Fares for those are $110, $165 and $250 from Liberia.

Can we go on to another destination after the stay?

Yes. From the resort we go to La Fortuna for $285, to Monteverde for $275 or to Nosara for $280, each for up to five passengers and collected at the entrance.

How do I book a transfer to Planet Hollywood Costa Rica?

To book online, type “Planet Hollywood Costa Rica” in the calculator (it appears under Papagayo), then choose your date and vehicle. You pay by card through Tilopay, and you can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before pickup.
