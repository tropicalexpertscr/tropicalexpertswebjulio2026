A La Fortuna visitor guide places Magic Mountain 2 km from the central park on the road to the volcano, which makes it the closest hotel to the centre among those we have pages for. HRS lists it as a three-star hotel. Its website is hotelmagicmountain.com.

Being so close to town has a practical side for transfers: arrival and departure times depend almost entirely on the airport leg. When we set a departure pickup, the minutes between the hotel and La Fortuna hardly count; the mountain roads to SJO or the lake road to Liberia are what we plan around.

From the airports

Getting to Hotel Magic Mountain from SJO and Liberia

From SJO airport (San José)

From SJO you head west on Route 1 and cross the mountains on Route 702 via San Ramón or Route 141 via Zarcero, reaching La Fortuna in approx. 3 h according to our fare table. Magic Mountain is 2 km from the park, a couple of minutes more. On VIP transfers we go over Vara Blanca for La Paz waterfall and Cinchona.

From Liberia airport (LIR)

From Liberia the route runs along the north shore of Lake Arenal and then down Route 142 toward La Fortuna. Magic Mountain is the last stretch before the centre on that side. Our fare table gives approx. 2 h 45 min of driving.

Before you go

Practical notes for Hotel Magic Mountain

  1. Close to town means an easy final evening: book your departure transfer and we set the pickup time around your flight.
  2. Heading to Tamarindo or the Papagayo Peninsula after Arenal? It is $315 or $285 for up to five passengers.
  3. Pack passports and anything you need for check-in in your hand luggage, not in the trunk.
  4. If your flight is delayed, you don't need to call: we track the flight number and adjust the pickup at no cost.

Questions

Questions about transfers to Hotel Magic Mountain

How much is a private shuttle from SJO or Liberia airport to Hotel Magic Mountain?

From SJO: $220 (Staria, 1–5 passengers), $275 (Hiace, 6–9) or $360 (Maxus, 10–12). From Liberia: $225, $280 or $365. Per vehicle, taxes included; the return costs the same.

How far is Magic Mountain from La Fortuna?

About 2 km from La Fortuna's central park, on the road toward Arenal Volcano.

Is there a surcharge for Magic Mountain?

No. It is in the La Fortuna fare zone: $220 from SJO and $225 from Liberia for up to five passengers, taxes included.

What time would we leave Magic Mountain for a noon flight at SJO?

Early in the morning. The usual advice is to be at the airport about 3 hours before an international flight; add the approx. 3 h drive and a margin for the 2026 roadworks on Route 1. We confirm the exact time when you book.

Can we make stops on the way from Liberia?

With VIP (+$80 per vehicle): the Lake Arenal viewpoints, a lookout above the water, Café y Macadamia and wildlife stops when animals appear by the road. Extra hours are $35 each, up to 4.

How do we pay?

Online by Visa, Mastercard or American Express on Tilopay's secure page when you book; SINPE Móvil or bank transfer can be arranged on WhatsApp. There is nothing to pay the driver.

Can you take us from Magic Mountain to Manuel Antonio?

Yes: $330 for up to five passengers, $375 in the Hiace and $460 in the Maxus V90. It is one of the longest trips from Arenal, so the time is in the table above.

Are the vehicles air-conditioned?

Yes. Our vans are recent units with air conditioning, WiFi and real luggage space, and bottled water is on board for every transfer.

How do I book a transfer to Hotel Magic Mountain?

To book online, type “Hotel Magic Mountain” in the calculator (it appears under La Fortuna · Arenal), then choose your date and vehicle. You pay by card through Tilopay, and you can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before pickup.
