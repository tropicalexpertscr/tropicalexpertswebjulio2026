Our story

The people behind the company

My name is Johnson Paniagua Berrocal, and Tropical Experts Costa Rica is the family company I founded in 2023 together with my wife and co-founder, Andrea Segura. We live in La Fortuna de San Carlos with our two daughters, at the foot of Arenal volcano, and from here we lead a team of professional drivers covering the whole country.

I have spent more than 21 years in tourism, working in all kinds of places: canopy guide at Sky Trek, rafting guide and naturalist guide. I then moved into five-star hotels such as Tabacón and Arenal Kioro, in guest reception, concierge and customer service, where I also trained new staff. I know what a traveler needs because I have worked in every link of their trip.

How we work

Why that matters on the road

There is one chapter of my life that defines how we work: I was a detective with the Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ) for 8 years. That is where the values this company does not negotiate come from — safety, discipline, attention to detail and punctuality.

For us, transporting someone is not moving them from one point to another: it is taking responsibility for our clients arriving safe, comfortable and on time.

Our mission is simple: to make your holiday the best it can be, and for you not merely to reach your destination, but to enjoy the road there.
