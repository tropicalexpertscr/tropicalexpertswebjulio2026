Decision

Which airport should you choose?

To reach La Fortuna, neither airport is the wrong answer. Pick the one with the better flight for you, then check these cases:

  • Your trip ends on a Guanacaste beach: Liberia

    Tamarindo is approx. 1 h 15 min from Liberia airport and approx. 4 h 45 min from SJO; Papagayo and Playas del Coco are approx. 30 min from Liberia. If your last nights are on that coast, flying home from LIR saves a long drive on departure day.

  • Next stop Manuel Antonio or Jacó: SJO

    Manuel Antonio is approx. 3 h from SJO and approx. 5 h from Liberia; Jacó is approx. 1 h 45 min from SJO and approx. 3 h 30 min from Liberia. On a Central Pacific trip, SJO is the closer airport for the flight home.

  • A night in San José: SJO

    Downtown San José is approx. 30 min from SJO (from $80 per vehicle) and approx. 4 h 30 min from Liberia.

  • Landing late: either one

    We pick up at both airports 24 hours a day and track your flight, so a late landing is not a problem. After dark both drives run a little slower: the mountain stretch from SJO is unlit and can be foggy, and the lake road from Liberia has almost no street lights. You can sleep on the way.

  • Monteverde comes next: either one

    Monteverde is approx. 2 h 45 min from Liberia and approx. 3 h 15 min from SJO, and approx. 3 h 15 min from La Fortuna ($255 per vehicle). Both airports fit a La Fortuna and Monteverde trip.

  • You want the scenic drive: your call

    From SJO the VIP stops are waterfalls and a mountain lookout; from Liberia the lake is the star. With VIP (+$80 per vehicle) either road becomes part of the holiday.

On the road

Two different drives to the same volcano

From SJO: over the mountains

The first stretch is Route 1, the busiest part of the trip, with 2026 roadworks between the airport and San Ramón. Then the road climbs into cool, often misty mountains and drops to the lowlands around La Fortuna, where Arenal appears if the clouds allow. Your driver picks Route 702 or Route 141 on the day, according to traffic and weather; VIP transfers go over Vara Blanca and Cinchona instead, for the waterfall stops.

VIP stops

  • La Paz waterfall and La Paz Waterfall Gardens
  • Restaurante Mirador Cinchona

From Liberia: around the lake

The first part is fast, flat highway across Guanacaste to Cañas. Past Tilarán, where wind turbines line the ridges, the road follows the north shore of Lake Arenal through Nuevo Arenal. It is paved but narrow and curvy, so the lake section sets the pace; in rain or after dark the trip gets closer to three hours.

VIP stops

  • Lake Arenal views
  • The lake lookout (mirador)
  • Café y Macadamia
  • Wildlife stops

FAQ

Questions about getting from the airport to La Fortuna

Is SJO or Liberia the better airport for La Fortuna?

Either works, and the transfer is nearly the same: $220 from SJO (approx. 3 h) or $225 from Liberia (approx. 2 h 45 min) for up to 5 passengers. Choose the airport with the better flights, then think about where your trip ends: Liberia suits the Guanacaste beaches, SJO suits Manuel Antonio, Jacó and San José.

How much is a private transfer from each airport to La Fortuna?

Per vehicle, taxes included. From SJO: $220 (1–5 passengers), $275 (6–9) or $360 (10–12). From Liberia: $225, $280 or $365. VIP stops add $80 per vehicle.

Which drive is shorter?

On the clock, Liberia: approx. 2 h 45 min against approx. 3 h from SJO, even though the distance is longer (about 157 km against about 125 km). The lake road from Liberia slows down in rain or after dark, and Route 1 out of SJO can be slow at rush hour, so in practice the two are very close.

Our flight lands late at night. Is one airport better?

No. We pick up at both airports 24 hours a day, track your flight and, if it is delayed, adjust the pickup at no extra cost. Both drives take a little longer in the dark, but you won't be the one driving.

Can we land at one airport and fly home from the other?

Yes, and it often saves a long drive at the end of the trip. La Fortuna to SJO is $220 and La Fortuna to Liberia $225 for up to 5 passengers, and if you finish on the coast there are direct transfers from the Guanacaste beaches to Liberia and from Manuel Antonio to SJO. Add every leg to the same cart and pay once.

Can we stop along the way?

Standard goes direct, with a restroom stop if you need one. With VIP (+$80 per vehicle) you get one to two hours of sightseeing stops: La Paz waterfall and Cinchona from SJO, or Lake Arenal lookouts and Café y Macadamia from Liberia. Extra hours are $35 each, up to 4.

Does the fare change if our hotel is outside La Fortuna town?

Most hotels are at the standard La Fortuna fare. Chateau Arenal is $20 more per vehicle; Arenal Observatory Lodge and Hotel Linda Vista (El Castillo) are $20 more from Liberia airport and $40 more from SJO airport. Arenal Lodge, Lost Iguana, Majestic Lodge, Essence Arenal and Rancho Margot are $40 more per vehicle, only on trips to or from SJO airport or San José.

How do we pay, and what if we cancel?

You pay online by card (Visa, Mastercard or American Express) on Tilopay's secure page; if you prefer SINPE Móvil or a bank transfer, we arrange it on WhatsApp. Cancel up to 48 hours before pickup for a full refund; within 48 hours there is no refund, except for a flight cancellation documented by the airline.
